Use paused only at the user's explicit request, never on your own initiative; a later resume revokes that request. Report returned status and stop goal work. Budget limits take precedence over pausing. Use blocked only when the same genuine blocker repeats for at least three consecutive goal turns and no meaningful progress is possible without user input or external-state change. A resumed blocked goal starts a fresh blocked audit. Once the threshold is met, mark blocked instead of leaving active. Difficulty, slow progress, uncertainty, incompleteness or useful clarification alone are not blockers. Do not mark complete because budget is nearly exhausted or you are stopping. You cannot resume, budget-limit or usage-limit via this tool. When completing a budgeted goal, report final token usage from the result.
